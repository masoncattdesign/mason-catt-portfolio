Amazon Business Payments · 2023

Pay Studio

An atomic design system for every Amazon Business payment method, built in three layers: components, templates made from them, and use cases wired from templates. One of the first shared libraries of its kind at Amazon, and the first in Amazon Business Payments.

47components, Accordion to Toast
80%adoption in 8 months
3layers, part to journey

Customer needs

          
Four questions

          
Research with 23 card members sorted their top 10 jobs into four questions: what do I owe, what are my benefits, where do I get help, and what’s happening with my account. The page is organized around them, in that order.
